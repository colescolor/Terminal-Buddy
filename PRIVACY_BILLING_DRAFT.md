# Future billing privacy draft — not published or approved
The current free release does not gain telemetry or a new account requirement through this work.
The future opt-in edition would send the account service only verified email/account identity, trial activation/status requests, device sign-in challenge/state, account-session credentials and billing actions. It does not send chats, code, terminal output, filenames, project paths, provider credentials or device fingerprints.
Supabase would handle email authentication and hold account records in Postgres. Stripe would collect payment details through its own hosted pages; Terminal Buddy would retain Stripe customer/subscription/Checkout references, paid-through state and event IDs, not card numbers or complete billing payloads.
Resend would deliver sign-in emails through Supabase's configured SMTP connection and process recipient addresses and message contents, including verification codes, under its service settings. Email delivery/log retention and regions need owner review. No marketing mailing list or open/click tracking is added.
The private owner dashboard would allow explicitly approved owner account IDs to view connected-account emails, trial dates, stored billing/access status and last reconciliation timestamps. It does not expose customer session tokens or card details, and does not change subscription records. Browser data is not deliberately persisted; sign-out clears the rendered records. Provider-administration access and any future staff permissions need review.
App account tokens/cache are held in OS-backed encrypted storage where supported, otherwise memory only. Supabase's browser session is memory-only. Hosting/auth/payment providers may process IP addresses and operational logs even though the application does not deliberately collect them as analytics.
Owner/legal review must specify controller/contact, purposes/legal basis as applicable, provider agreements and regions, retention/deletion/access requests, backup retention, billing-record requirements, security incident handling, refund/tax rules and private support. No compliance certification or absolute privacy guarantee is made.
No analytics, advertising pixels, session replay or desktop telemetry has been added. Production logging configuration must exclude Authorization headers, OTPs, request bodies and payment payloads. Trial/account history needed to prevent reset must have an approved retention/deletion policy; do not promise unlimited deletion or indefinite retention without that decision.
